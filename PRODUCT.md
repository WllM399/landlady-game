# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, confirmed by the user. The project should open directly in a browser.

## Users

The author is a student preparing a literature assignment. Players are classmates or other readers who should be able to follow the story without controlling a moving character.

## Product Purpose

An accessible point-and-click story game retells Roald Dahl's "The Landlady" and joins it smoothly to the student's own continuation. Success means a player can explore the key locations, understand the story in order, and revisit discovered events in an in-game notebook.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Players click between rooms and locations; they do not move a character directly.
- The story is revealed in sequence as players investigate and progress.
- A notebook lets players revisit the story information they have uncovered.
- The script should cover the most important scenes from beginning to end and preserve the supplied plot and ending.
- Do not add major plot events that are absent from the source text or the student's continuation.
- Use clear, readable source code suitable for a school project.
- The game interface and story text are in German.

## Brand Commitments

The user asked for a hand-drawn, lightly eerie picture-book mood. Deponia is a reference for point-and-click interaction only; its characters and graphics are not to be reused.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the author's plot and character motives.
- Make exploration and story progression easy to understand.
- Let players revisit discovered information.
- Keep the code legible to a beginner.
